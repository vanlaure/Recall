## Learner content

Text, audio and photos a learner submits are theirs. Audio is sent to the speech provider and not kept. A face photo
is sent to the vision model with an instruction not to identify the person, stored in that learner's private library,
and deleted with the kit. Provider terms govern what the providers do with transmitted content; the README says so.
